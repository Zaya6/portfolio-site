# Preproduction 02 — Positioning Brief

_Decisions locked 2026-08-11._

## Who this site is for
**Two audiences, equal weight:**
1. **Art commission clients** — hire Isaiah to create art.
2. **Studios / contract clients** — hire Isaiah for technical-art & engineering contracts.

Design implication: the site needs to serve both without diluting either. Likely a shared front door with two clear paths (art track / tech track), not two disconnected sites.

## What he wants to be hired for (services menu)
- Digital art / illustration (2D concepts, character/environment, digital painting)
- 3D art & modeling (characters, environments, objects, renders)
- 3D rigging (character rigs, armatures, IK/FK)
- Shader / tech-art work (shaders, VFX, tools, pipeline automation)
- Debugging & optimization (GPU profiling, performance, engineering rigor)
- Full game contracts (end-to-end: design, code, art, ship)

This is a wide range — spans pure-artist to senior-engineer. The site must make it legible that **one person credibly covers all of it** (that's the differentiator, not a liability).

## Build approach
**Custom-coded.** The site is itself a portfolio piece. Opportunities to demonstrate craft live: shader/WebGL hero or accents, motion, deliberate typography, interactive project pieces. Bar: the site should look like a senior Technical Artist made it.

## Positioning / lead credential
**Blend both** — "Senior Technical Artist who is also a shipping indie developer and digital artist." 25 years coding. AAA-adjacent studio depth (Light & Wonder — *Kong: Skull Island*; Everi TA IV) **plus** independent range (own engines, shipped games, original art). The claim to land: rare full-stack range from pixel to pipeline.

## Positioning risks to manage
- **Jack-of-all-trades dilution.** Wide service menu can read as unfocused. Mitigate with a strong unifying identity + case studies that show depth, not just breadth.
- **Two audiences, one nav.** Must not make an art client wade through shader tooling, or a studio wade through illustration. Clear pathing.
- **Craft bar.** A custom site that looks mediocre undercuts the whole pitch. Design investment is non-optional here.
