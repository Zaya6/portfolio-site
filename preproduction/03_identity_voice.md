# Preproduction 03 — Identity, Story & Voice

_From interview 2026-08-11. His words preserved; distillation below is working material for copy, not final copy._

---

## Narrative spine (the throughline)
Lifelong maker — game design, games, and **narrative** have been the constant since childhood. Most of his art originates from a personal project where he "sought to realize an experience of beauty," each carrying a **deep backstory and atmosphere**. He strives to find **beauty in the simple experiences of life**. He frames the act of creating new art / stories / experiences as **akin to building life on earth itself — taking part in the grand order of creation by a higher creative power.**

> The work is sacred; he is not. Reverent about creation, unpretentious about himself. That tension is the voice.

## Core method — *shibumi* as practice (not decoration)
Rejects "big bombastic set pieces." Believes real complexity lives in **simple things** — "the mundane has extreme beauty within it for those who look." His method: **take a single idea — a motif or a style — and filter it to its purest form.** Consistent across all three disciplines:
- **Story:** distill to a pure motif
- **Code:** values **simplicity and elegance**
- **Art:** focuses on **the lives depicted** (people/atmosphere over spectacle)

This is the unifying thesis that keeps the wide service menu from reading as scattered: *one sensibility — purity through subtraction — applied to code, art, and story alike.*

## What the range means (proudest-of)
Proudest of **breadth + adaptability.** Roles held across a lifetime: event organizer, mural painter, entrepreneur, VFX artist, technical artist, 3D artist, digital painter, documentation writer. **Desired takeaway:** "a person of great depth with the talent to learn and adapt to work in any area." Breadth is framed as *depth + learning ability*, not dabbling.

## The superpower (differentiator)
**Visualization.** Gets a **strong, usually-correct vision right from the start** of a task. "When I close my eyes I can teleport my inner eye to alien worlds." Crucially, visualizes **math**: sees mathematical operations transform things visually in his head before writing code. → This is the literal bridge between artist and engineer — the thing a pure artist *and* a pure engineer each lack. **Lead the "blend both" claim with this.**

## Self-taught
**Completely self-taught**, over a lifetime of practice, life experience, and hard work. Wants to **lean into this**. Frame as earned mastery, not credentials.

## Who he wants to reach
- **Ideal clients:** indie game teams, studio heads, art directors.
- **Dream commission:** a film, or a studio like **Remedy Entertainment** — the kind of project he'd clear his calendar for.

## Voice & tone
**Reference persona: Ryan Gosling.** Doesn't take life too seriously; down-to-earth, approachable — but quietly, genuinely talented and impressive. Register:
- **Serious about the work, light about the self.** Never narcissistic, never self-important. Human first.
- Warm, grounded, approachable. Confident without posturing.
- Dry humor welcome; reverence reserved for the craft, not the résumé.
- Avoid: hype adjectives, buzzword salad, "rockstar/ninja" self-branding, corporate stiffness.
