I was brought onto Big Pig to build the character rig for our lead artist. I created it in Maya with IK/FK switching and a meta rig driven by custom control shapes.

## The rig

I built the IK/FK switching itself in Maya's node editor, wiring up custom math to blend cleanly between the IK and FK controls rather than leaning on a canned solution. On top of that I built a meta rig for the character — a higher-level layer of custom control shapes that made it comfortable to pose. Beyond the core setup, I added morphs and bones to give the character some personality — including a dedicated bone that let the pig shake its hips. I optimized the topology for animation and did extensive weight painting to achieve the most natural deformations possible, so the rig held up cleanly under an animator's hands.

I had not built an IK/FK switch rig in Maya before. It had been flagged as a wish-list feature, and I took it on as a personal challenge. We were under real time pressure with GDC approaching — without the rig, the game could not be shown — and I finished it in time to be animated and presented at the conference. I include Big Pig here as an example of showing up for the team and going beyond what is expected.
